# Technology Operations Framework

## Purpose

The Technology Operations Framework establishes a structured approach for delivering reliable, secure, and scalable technology services while aligning operational performance with business objectives.

---

## Strategic Objectives

- Maintain high availability of critical technology services
- Deliver consistent and measurable service levels
- Reduce operational risk through proactive governance
- Improve operational efficiency through automation
- Foster continuous improvement across technology teams
- Support business growth with scalable operational processes

---

## Core Operational Pillars

### Service Delivery

- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Operational performance monitoring
- Customer communication
- Service ownership

### Incident Management

- Rapid incident response
- Root cause analysis
- Major incident coordination
- Executive communications

### Problem Management

- Trend analysis
- Preventative improvements
- Knowledge management
- Continuous process optimization

### Change Management

- Risk assessment
- CAB approvals
- Change validation
- Post-implementation review

### Operational Governance

- KPI reporting
- Executive dashboards
- Risk management
- Compliance oversight

---

## Success Metrics

- SLA Compliance
- Incident Resolution Time
- Mean Time to Restore Service (MTTR)
- Customer Satisfaction
- Change Success Rate
- Operational Efficiency
- Service Availability

---

## Continuous Improvement

Technology Operations should continually evaluate performance, identify improvement opportunities, and implement automation and process enhancements that improve reliability, scalability, and customer experience.
